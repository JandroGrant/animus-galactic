*Influence 13 [[Spells#QuickSpells|Quick Spell]]* \
**Cost:** 1 Energy \
**Risk:** 1 Energy, if no Energy can be lost, gain 2 Stress then Double Stress \
**Range:** Sight

One target within range loses all Block if they have less than 20 Block. If they have more than 20 Block, or have no Block, they gain 2 Stacks of [[Aflame]].
