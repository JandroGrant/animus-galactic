*Mechanics 11 [[Spells#Held Spells|Held Spell]]* \
**Cost:** 3 Energy \
**Risk:** 3 Stress \
**Range:** 30 ft

Whenever you gain Block, deal 2 Damage to one combatant within range. If this spell is ended by breaking the Held Spell, deal damage equal to your Stress to all combatants within 5 ft (after you gain the Stress from the risk).
