*Mechanics 13 [[Spells#Quick Spells|Quick Spell]]* \
**Cost:** 1 Energy or 1 Reaction, an open hand \
**Risk:** 1 Stress \
**Range:** Sight

Move one item into your open hand. The item cannot be held by another creature or combatant and must fit in your hand. If there is not a direct path to your hand, the item moves 5 ft closer instead.
