*Technology 12 [[Spells#Held Spells|Held Spell]]* \
**Cost:** 2 Energy \
**Risk:** none \
**Range:** Self

You shroud yourself in a thin, shimmering shell. While this Spell is held, you gain an amount of Block equal to your Intelligence Stat at the end of your turn.
