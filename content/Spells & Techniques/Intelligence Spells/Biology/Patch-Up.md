*Biology 13 [[Spells#Quick Spells|Quick Spell]]* \
**Cost:** 2 Energy \
**Risk:** 2 Stress \
**Range:** 10 ft

Heal an ally for 4d4 HP + your Biology skill and reduce their Stress by 1. If they are at or below 0 HP afterwards, but not above their Stress limit, they are brought back up to 1 HP.
