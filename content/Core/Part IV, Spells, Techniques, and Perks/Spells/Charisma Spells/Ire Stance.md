*Influence 7 Quick Spell*
**Cost:** 1 Energy
**Risk:** None
**Range:** n/a

Spend one energy to enter the "Rage" stance.

Stances are a type of Condition that you can only have one of at a time. If you enter a new stance while in a stance already, you exit your previous stance and enter the new one.

While in Rage, all attacks made against you are Critical Hits. In return, the first two attacks made during your turn cost one less energy.

Casting this spell while in Rage causes you to exit Rage instead of entering it.
