*Biology 12 Quick Spell*
**Cost:** 1 Energy
**Risk:** 2 Stress
**Range:** n/a

Spend one energy to enter the "Bliss" stance. 

Stances are a type of Condition that you can only have one of at a time. If you enter a new stance while in a stance already, you exit your previous stance and enter the new one.

The first time you exit the Bliss stance during your turn, gain one energy and reset your reaction.
