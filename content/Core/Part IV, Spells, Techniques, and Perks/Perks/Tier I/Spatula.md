The first time you roll dice with size less than 10 (d4, d6, d8) each turn, you may flip any of the dice upside down. If you are using a digital dice roller, you may use the following formula the determine the new value:

Where $$s$$ is the size of the die, and $$v$$ is the value of the roll,

$$s - v + 1$$

If you use 2 d10s to roll a d100, count the value as one die. Note that some d10s have their highest value as 9, in such cases use the formula instead of flipping.
