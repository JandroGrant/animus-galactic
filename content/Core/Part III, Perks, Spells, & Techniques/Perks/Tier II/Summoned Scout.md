After a full-rest, can spend one Turret Slot and 10 Scrap to summon a simple robotic scout that will assist you throughout the day. The scout can take any appearance so long as the size is no larger than a 2 ft cube.

The scout can have one of the abilities from the following table at your choice:

| Ability Name    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Guard D.O.G.    | Whenever you successfully land a Melee Attack, the scout will move 20 ft towards the target and attempt to deal damage to the target. If the attack has multiple targets, the scout will attack the target with the most health.<br><br>If the scout is not within 20 ft of the target, the scout will instead move as close to you as it can get within those 20 ft.<br><br>The damage dealt by the scout is equal to its health. |
| Hover Sentry    | When you land a successful Ranged Attack, the scout will deal 2 damage to all targets affected.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Adorable Friend | The scout grants +2 to all Influence checks aimed to charm.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Mini-Mule       | The scout has a movement speed of 50 ft. You can spend 1 Energy to send the scout anywhere within its range. The scout can carry one item no larger than a 1 ft cube. The scout cannot pick up any items, items must be willingly given to it.                                                                                                                                                                                     |

The scout starts with an amount of health equal to your Biology Skill. If the scout drops below 1 HP, it shatters into 8 Scrap.
