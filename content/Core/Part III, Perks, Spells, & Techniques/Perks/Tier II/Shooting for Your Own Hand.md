Whenever you roll doubles, you gain the ability to shoot down the next ranged attack made against you, causing the roll to have a negative modifier equal to your Reflex Skill. In order to do so, you must have a Ranged Weapon in hand and you must spend a Reaction.

This effect stacks such that each stack may be used on different projectiles. You can never use multiple stacks on one Attack Roll. Stacks do not persist between combats.
