# Skills

Skills in the world of Animus are a representation of a character's abilities and qualities; how well they interact with the world around them. Skills influence all aspects of game-play from combat ability to social interaction and everything in between.
## All Skills
- Strength:
	- Athletics:
		- Measures all physical acts of movement.
		- Almost all involved movement actions use Athletics.
	- Endurance:
		- Measures ones fortitude, their ability to resist.
		- Indirectly reduces HP loss in combat.
	- Might:
		- Measures force and physical intimidation.
		- Necessary for combat if melee attacks are a focus.
- Dexterity:
	- Handiwork:
		- Measures acts of working with the hands and proficiency with tools.
		- Useful for repairing damaged weapons and tools.
	- Reflex:
		- Measures one's gut instinct. Their ability to dodge or pilot.
		- Necessary for combat if ranged attacks are a focus.
	- Stealth:
		- Measures one's ability to remain undetected, to be sneaky.
		- Hopefully self-explanatory.
- Intelligence:
	- Biology:
		- The understanding of the various fauna and medicines around you.
	- Mechanics:
		- The understanding of the physical world. How forces interact and change.
	- Technology:
		- The understanding of the digital world. Computing, lines of code, and hacking. You never need to leave the terminal.
- Charisma:
	- Influence:
		- Measures the ability to influence others.
		- The power of using words to gain the upper hand. Necessary for social interaction.
		- Represents intimidation with words.
	- Intuition:
		- Measures social awareness and understanding. The ability to read the room.
	- Performance:
		- Measures the ability to perform.
		- Includes musical ability and general showmanship.
