# Attacking

Not all conflict can be avoided, eventually your characters may need to attack enemies. Attacking in Animus is relatively straight-forward.
## Attacks and Damage

By default, all attacks in Animus hit automatically. No rolls are required to hit, only for damage. The main exception to this is the "Evasion" buff, covered in [[13. Defending|the next Chapter]].

As specified in [[6. Health Points & Block|Chapter 6, Health Points & Block]], HP loss and Damage are different in Animus. Almost all attacks deal Damage instead of direct HP loss. An Attack's damage is denoted as the following pattern,

*"Dice Roll" "Attack Type" "(optional) Skill Bonus" "(optional) Damage Modifiers" Damage*

a few examples include:

*1d6 Melee Might Damage \
1d12 Ranged Handiwork Damage \
4d4 Ranged Handiwork Barrage Damage \
2d6 Spell Damage \
1d8 Spell Influence Damage* 

The "Dice Roll" of the attack represents the dice rolled to start the damage, the "Attack Type" represents the behavior of the attack, the "Skill Bonus" represents the skill to be added to the total damage, and the "Damage Modifiers" represent any additional ways the damage should behave. Note that not all damage has an associated Skill Bonus or Damage Modifier.
### Attack Types

The three main Attack Types are "Melee", "Ranged", and "Spell" attacks. Each behaving slightly different from the other
#### Melee Attacks

Melee Attacks are any type of physical attack. Examples include swords, axes, knives, flails, staffs, etc. Melee Attacks on a combatant with the "Thorns" buff take damage equal to the Thorns.
#### Ranged Attacks

Ranged Attacks are any type of projectile-based attack. Examples includes firearms, bow and arrows, thrown weapons, etc. Ranged attacks do not trigger Thorns damage.
#### Spell Attacks

Spell Attacks are more akin to Spells than the other Attack types. Each Spell description for Spell attacks will explain the contexts for each attack and will typically align it closer to either a Melee Attack, or a Ranged Attack.
### Damage Modifiers

Damage Modifiers modify aspects of the attack or damage dealt by weapons. All current modifiers are listed below.
#### Barrage

Each dice rolled for Barrage Damage counts as separate instances of damage without affecting the total damage. For example, damage labeled as:

*4d4 Ranged Reflex Barrage Damage*

will deal 1d4 damage, 4 times, then deal additional damage equal to the Reflex bonus.
#### Piercing

Piercing damage pierces through Block, dealing direct damage to HP while still counting as Attack Damage. See [[6. Health Points & Block| Chapter 6, Health Points & Block]] for more details.
### Critical Damage

In most games with Critical Damage, the opportunity to land a Critical hit is determined alongside rolling to hit. However, since all attacks land by default in Animus, Critical Damage functions differently.

When rolling for Attack Damage, each die that rolls its highest possible value grants you another roll of the same die. For example, if an attack has one player roll 3d4, and 2 out of the 3 d4s roll 4, they get to roll 2 more d4s. This effect repeats until no dice have rolled their highest value.

For the Barrage Damage Modifier, these extra rolls do not count as extra hits.
