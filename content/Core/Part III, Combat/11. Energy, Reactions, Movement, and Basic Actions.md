## Basic Actions

### Brace (1)
#Action

Spend 1 Energy to gain Block equal to your Endurance Skill.
### Breathe (2)
#Action 

Spend 2 Energy to destress by an amount equal to your Stress Limit. You must be within an environment that allows you to breathe.
### Consume (1) or Consume (R)  
#Action 

Spend 1 Energy or 1 Reaction to consume one consumable.
### Crawl (1) 
#Action #Action/Movement

Spend 1 Energy to move 5 ft while Prone. Dropping Prone is not included in this action.
### Climb (1) 
#Action #Action/Movement 

Spend 1 Energy to climb 5 ft vertically up a wall or surface.
### Drop (1)
#Action #Action/Movement 

Drop Prone. While prone, all Stealth rolls are made with Imprecision.
### Escape Grasp (1)
#Action #Action/Movement 

Once per turn, you can spend 1 Energy to try and escape from a grapple. Make an Athletics versus Might roll from the combatant that is grappling you. One a success, you escape their grasp and move 5 ft.
### Feed (1)
#Action 

Spend 1 Energy to feed one consumable to a willing combatant. The consumable must be in your inventory or the target's hands.
### Grapple (1)
#Action #Action/Attack

Spend 1 Energy to make a Might versus Athletics roll against one combatant. On a success, the target is held within your grasp and is unable to move or take any movement action other than [[#Escape Grasp (1)]].

### Leap (1)
#Action #Action/Movement 

Spend 1 Energy to long jump forward up to 10 ft naturally, or, roll for Athletics. If the roll is more than or equal to the distance (in ft) that you wish to clear, you clear the jump. Otherwise, you jump 10 ft forward as usual.
### Loot (1)
#Action 

Spend 1 Energy to search through an inventory to discover the items within. With this action you can roll for Intuition if you wish to do more than a surface level search.
### Anticipate (2)
#Action 

Call out an Action that a specific combatant will take before your next turn. If that specific combatant makes that specific Action, you may use your Reaction to complete any Action that costs 1 Energy after they complete that Action.
### Stand Up (1)
#Action #Action/Movement 

Spend 1 Energy to stand up from Prone.
### Swim (1)
#Action #Action/Movement 

Spend 1 Energy to move 10 ft through water.
