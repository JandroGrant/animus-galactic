*Requirements:*
- A Mech with a Tier 1 Hull

When you pull your release lever, you can choose for your Mech to produce a thick cloud of steam that heats up your surroundings. While you are surrounded by the steam cloud, the Mech gain +2 AC for each Tier of your Mech's Hull. At the start of your next turn, the steam cloud dissipates.

If your Block is [[Block & HP|broken]] while the Steam Barrier is up, whomever broke the Block gains 1d6 stacks of the [[Aflame]] status.
