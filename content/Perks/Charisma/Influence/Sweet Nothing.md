*Requirements:*
- Influence 1

As a reaction, you can verbally encourage your allies to help improve their abilities. Whenever an ally is about to roll a Skill Check, you can give them 1d4 to add to the roll, potentially causing it to succeed. Note that this must be done before the roll is made.

If the roll is a Spell Roll that ends up failing, all negative risks are negated.

You can use this perk up to a maximum of 3 times per day.
