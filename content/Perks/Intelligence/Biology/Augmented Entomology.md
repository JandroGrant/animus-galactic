*Requirements:*
- Biology 9

Every time you're about to wake up, you begin to lucid dream of a significant room in your character's past. The exits from that room have been replaced with five unique doors:
- An ornate spruce door swathed with hundreds of Monarch Butterflies, each flapping regally, promising fortunate Charisma.
- A large boulder door, resembling that of a tomb entrance. Behind the door you can hear the crumbling of large rocks.
- A door made of fine sands that you could easily claw through. Behind the sand you can hear a seemingly infinite buzzing.
- A large glistening honeycomb door. Ethereal, steaming honey oozing from each hexagon cell. Behind this door is also a loud buzzing.
- A simple wooden door with vines climbing up the hinged side. In the center is a simple painting of a ladybug on a leaf.

Opening one of these doors wakes you up and grants the corresponding buff and appearance change (according to the following table) to your [[Neurons]] until you next fall asleep. Alternatively, you can choose to wake up without opening one of the doors; doing so will cause your neurons to adapt the look of regular neurons with no extra abilities.

| Door           | Appearance       | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :------------- | :--------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Ornate Door    | Butterfly Neuron | Your neurons now deal no damage, instead, each neuron adds +1 to your Charisma rolls for every 2 Butterflies. After each Charisma roll, all butterflies fly away, granting you 3 [[Block & HP\|Block]].                                                                                                                                    |
| Tomb Door      | Scarab Neuron    | Each neuron sent out deals 2 Damage instead of just 1. If the scarab swarm consists of 8 or more neurons, the target must succeed a DC 14 Endurance check or fall prone.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Sandy Door     | Locust Neuron    | Only one locust neuron can be sent out at a time. Locust neurons attach themselves to targets and remain on the target between rounds. If the target changes or falls unconscious, or you fall unconscious, all locusts die. The swarm deals 1 Damage at the start of your turn for each locust. There can only be one target at one time. |
| Honeycomb Door | Wasp Neuron      | Your neurons now deal no damage. After obtaining 6 wasp neurons, all wasps die and your next attack guarantees a critical hit. This effect does not stack.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Simple Door    | Ladybug Neuron   | Your neurons now deal no damage. Every 4 ladybugs heal 1 [[Block & HP\|HP]] for the target, then disappear.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
