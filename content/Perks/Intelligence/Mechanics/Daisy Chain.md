*Requirements:*
- Mechanics 6

The first time you hit a combatant with an attack on your turn, roll a d6. On a 5 or a 6, half the damage (rounded down) is also dealt to any one target within 10 ft. Repeat this effect until you do not roll a 5 or a 6. This effect cannot hit the same target twice. Each d6 roll after the first counts as rolling doubles.

*Repeat:*
- *This effect can hit the same target at most twice.*
