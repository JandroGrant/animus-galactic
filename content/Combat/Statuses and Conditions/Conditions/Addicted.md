After reaching the addiction level of a specific drug, you become Addicted. Addiction can be postponed by consuming a drug again. Addictions last between combats and can only be removed with an 8-hour rest.

While you are Addicted, you double your [[Stress|stress]] at the end of your turn if it is not 0. If your [[Stress|stress]] is at 0, gain 2 [[Stress]].
