Many effects in Animus rely on or directly change  your character's Stress Level. Alongside your stress level is your Stress Limit. Each point of stress past your stress limit is subtracted from all DC checks, this includes but is not limited to:
- Attack Rolls
- [[Stats, Skills, and Dice Checks|Skill Checks]]
- [[Spells#Casting Spells]]

Stress can be reduced with the Breathe Action and other common effects.
